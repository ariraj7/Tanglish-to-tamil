    How it works

    1. Read the longest piece first

      The translator reads your text from left to right and always takes the longest piece it recognises, so zh becomes ழ instead of a z followed by an h, and aa becomes the long ா.

    2. Build each letter from two parts

      Most Tamil letters are a consonant plus a vowel sign: க and ி make கி. A consonant with no vowel after it gets a dot, the pulli: க்.

    3. Check where the letter sits

      Tamil spelling depends on position. An n at the start of a word is ந, in the middle it is ன, and before d it is ண. A t that starts a word is த, because Tamil words don't begin with ட.

    4. Compare with real words

      English letters can't show every Tamil sound, so l might mean ல, ள or ழ. For each word, Ari tries small changes like these and offers the ones found in a list of about 17,600 Tamil words, most common first.
