Typing tips

  • Long vowels: double the letter. a is அ, aa is ஆ; i is இ, ee is ஈ.
  • Capitals matter for a few letters: N ண, L ள, R ற, E ஏ, O ஓ. Other capitals are read as lowercase.
  • One n, three letters: n gives ந at the start of a word, ன in the middle, and ண before t or d.
  • Doubled sounds: type the letter twice. kk gives க்க, as in வணக்கம்.
  • Not sure? Type it the way you'd text a friend. If the word isn't recognised, the translator suggests the closest real word.
